Privacy Policy

IBO Pro Smarter for Android and Android TV · Last updated: 9 October 2026

العربية

In short. IBO Pro Smarter is a media player. It contains no channels, films or other content of its own: you add your own playlist. A small demo of openly licensed test videos is included so you can try the app first.

The app has no account of its own, no advertising and no analytics. What you enter stays on your device and is sent only to the playlist provider you chose.

Who is responsible

IBO Pro Smarter is published by Yalla Max Pro (“we”). For any question about this policy, write to iboprosmarter@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your device

None of this is sent to us. We run no server that receives data from the app, so we cannot see your playlist, what you watch, or any of your settings.

The app's data is excluded from Android's cloud backup.

What leaves your device, and to whom

  • Your playlist provider. To load the playlist and play a stream, the app connects to the server you entered and sends it the login you entered. As with any internet connection, that server sees your IP address and which streams you request. The provider is chosen by you and is not operated by us; its own privacy policy applies to what it does with that information.
  • Image hosts named by your playlist. Channel logos and posters are downloaded from the addresses your playlist gives. The servers at those addresses see your IP address when an image is fetched.
  • The hosts of the demo videos, only if you play the demo. “Try the demo” adds a short playlist that ships inside the app. Playing one of its videos downloads it from the public test server that hosts it (test-streams.mux.dev, demo.unified-streaming.com or storage.googleapis.com), which sees your IP address. No login is sent.
  • Another player app, only if you turn it on. If you choose “External player” in Settings, the address of the stream you open is handed to the player app you select. That address may contain your playlist login. The option is off by default.

The app does not contact any other server. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking component.

Permissions

The app asks for one permission: internet access, to reach your playlist provider. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or phone identity.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • Sign out (Settings → Sign out) erases that playlist's login, its stored lists, favourites and history from the device.
  • Clear history (Settings → Clear history) erases the watch history alone.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android's settings, erases everything the app stored.

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete or export on request. Anything held by your playlist provider must be requested from that provider.

Children

The app is a general-purpose player and is not directed at children. It has no content of its own. The parental PIN lets an adult lock or hide categories of the playlist they added.

Content and its providers

We do not supply, host, sell or endorse any playlist, channel or stream. You are responsible for having the right to access the content of the playlist you add.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy (for example, if a later version adds a feature that sends data anywhere new), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
